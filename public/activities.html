FIGHT PRACTICE!

Since 2002, the Argent Citadel of Halazar has welcomed medievalists of all sorts to our community and events.

ARMORED COMBAT PRACTICE-
SUNDAYS 1:30-4:30 - Gas Works Park, 2101 N Northlake Way, Seattle WA 98103

All are welcome to come and participate. We focus on EMP style rattan combat. If you have participated in other Medieval groups, please come and check us out. We are happy to share our style and learn from you!

Our focus is weekly improvement of skills, both melee and individual combat.

We encourage new and interested people to come and try it out. We have loaner gear for many shapes and sizes. Please message in advance to arrange for appropriate gear to borrow.
